## Operating Context

The app the household uses every day, at the till and one-handed: quick entry,
the period summary, Historial with the bank-charge inbox, Tarjetas (statement
check-off and the credit card's bank charges), Servicios, Ajustes. Opening and
closing card statements, typing a card charge by hand and editing fees stay on
the web. Installed by free-account sideload, so a build can be a week old on
the phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dynamic Type up to the accessibility sizes (layouts restack rather than
truncate), VoiceOver labels on every control, light and dark mode.
